# Combine

{command:model.combine} joins, cuts or intersects bodies. Select a face or edge of each of two
bodies, or choose two bodies in the tree, then choose Combine. The one earlier in the tree is the
target and the other the tool.

- **Operation**: Join adds the tool to the target, Cut takes it away, Intersect keeps what they
  share.
- **Target** and **Tool** can be changed in the panel; **Also with** adds more tool bodies.
- **Keep tool** leaves the tool bodies standing instead of using them up.

Cuts made while modelling are often simpler with an extrusion set to Remove from body; see
[Extrude](extrude).
