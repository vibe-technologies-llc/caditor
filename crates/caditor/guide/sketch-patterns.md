# Patterns in a sketch

Two tools repeat the selected geometry, keeping the copies in step with the original. Select the
geometry first. Neither has a key or a button; use the Sketch menu or {command:palette}.

## Rectangular pattern

{command:sketch.rectangular_pattern} repeats along one or two directions. Type the count and the
spacing in the field that opens:

- `4 x 10` gives four in all, the original included, 10 apart.
- `4 x 10, 3 x 15` adds three rows 15 apart.
- `3 x 12 < 45` runs along a direction at 45 degrees.

## Circular pattern

{command:sketch.circular_pattern} repeats about a point. Select that point with the geometry, or
click it after starting the tool. Then type:

- `6` to space six, the original included, over a full turn.
- `4 over 90` to spread four across 90 degrees.

The preview follows what you type, and Enter makes the copies. Spacings and angles may be
[expressions](expressions). For patterns of bodies and features, see [Patterns](patterns).
