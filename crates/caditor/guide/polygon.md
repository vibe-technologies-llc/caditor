# Polygon

{command:sketch.polygon} draws a regular polygon, all sides equal. It has three ways of drawing:

- {command:sketch.polygon.corner}: click the centre, then a corner.
- {command:sketch.polygon.side_middle}: click the centre, then the middle of a side, which sizes
  it across its flats.
- {command:sketch.polygon.side}: click the two ends of one side.

## Number of sides

{command:sketch.more_sides} and {command:sketch.fewer_sides} change the count, or type it in the
point field, such as `8 sides`. Holding Shift while drawing and moving the pointer sideways changes
the count too; release Shift to carry on. The count is kept for the next polygon.

The corner menu of the Polygon button also holds [Spur gear](gear), which draws the outline of a
gear.
