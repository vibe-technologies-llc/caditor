# Circle

{command:sketch.circle} draws circles. It has three ways of drawing:

- {command:sketch.circle.center}: click the centre, then a point on the circle.
- {command:sketch.circle.two_points}: click the two ends of a diameter.
- {command:sketch.circle.three_points}: click three points the circle passes through.

Pressing the tool's key again while it is active steps to the next way; the small menu on the
ribbon button lists them all.

The circle's size shows as you draw. A [Smart dimension](dimensions) on a circle gives its
diameter. For a circle touching other curves, see [Tangent circle](tangent-circle).
