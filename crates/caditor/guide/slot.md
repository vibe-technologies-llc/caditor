# Slot

{command:sketch.slot} draws a slot: two straight sides joined by round ends. It has three ways of
drawing:

- {command:sketch.slot.ends}: click the centres of the two round ends, then a point setting the
  width.
- {command:sketch.slot.center}: click the middle of the slot, the centre of one end, then the
  width; the slot stays centred.
- {command:sketch.slot.arc}: a curved slot along an arc. Click the arc's centre, the centres of
  the two ends, then the width.

Pressing the tool's key again while it is active steps to the next way. The length and the width
show beside the pointer as you draw.
