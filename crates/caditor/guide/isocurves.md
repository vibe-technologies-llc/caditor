# Isocurves

{command:view.isocurves} draws lines running across the selected faces, each with a
[curvature comb](curvature-comb), to show how a surface bends in each direction.

- **Lines along** chooses the U direction, the V direction or both.
- **Lines each way** sets how many lines; **Teeth per line** and **Scale** shape the combs.

The panel lists each face with the smallest radius of its lines in each direction. Selecting
something that is not a face keeps the faces shown. The lines are worked out in the background and
redrawn after every change to the model.
