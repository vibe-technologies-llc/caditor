# Point

{command:sketch.point} places single points. Click in the view, or type a position such as
`20, 10`.

A point placed on a curve stays on it, held by a Coincident constraint. That is how to mark where a
curve should be split ({command:sketch.split_curve}), where a [hole](hole) is drilled, or a datum a
[dimension](dimensions) measures from. Points are never part of a region's outline.

See also [snapping](snapping).
